本地乐谱 / Local scores

PDF、预览、识别结果、文件名、本地路径、曲谱库和转换日志保存在您的电脑。官方版本不向账号服务上传乐谱，也不把乐谱用于训练。账号验证需要联网；识别在本机执行。您主动向客服发送的资料由您自行选择，请先删除无关个人信息。

PDFs, previews, results, filenames, local paths, the score library and conversion logs stay on your computer. The official app does not upload scores to the account service or use them for training. Account verification needs internet access; recognition runs locally. Share support materials only when necessary and remove unrelated personal information first.

账号与安全数据 / Account and security data

我们保存用户名、已验证的注册邮箱、随机账号标识、密码的加盐 PBKDF2 摘要、恢复码摘要、注册时间、试用到期时间、同意的条款版本、会话摘要和授权状态。服务器不保存明文密码、明文恢复码、原始授权码或银行卡资料。Cloudflare 会处理访问 IP、请求时间等基础网络数据；应用将 IP 与秘密值组合后摘要，用于短期频率限制，不在业务数据库保存原始 IP。

We store your username, verified registration email, random account ID, salted PBKDF2 password hash, recovery-code hash, registration/trial dates, accepted terms version, session-token hashes and entitlement status. Our server stores no plaintext passwords, recovery codes, original activation codes or card details. Cloudflare processes network information such as IP addresses and request times. Application rate limits use a salted IP digest rather than raw IP addresses in the business database.

开放购买后，Creem 作为记录商户处理支付、适用税费、发票及相关退款。我们向 Creem 提供订单请求标识与内部账号标识，用于将订单绑定到账号；接收并核验订单、交易、退款及争议通知。Creem 可按其条款向商户提供买家与交易信息；本服务当前仅将履约所需的订单标识、产品标识和状态写入业务数据库，完整卡号不经过本服务。

When purchasing opens, Creem acts as merchant of record for payment, applicable taxes, invoices and related refunds. We send checkout-request and internal account IDs to link the purchase to your account, and verify order, payment, refund and dispute notifications. Creem may provide buyer and transaction information under its policies; this service currently persists only necessary order/product IDs and status. Full card numbers do not pass through our service.

用途与处理基础 / Purposes and legal bases

账号及授权数据用于履行账号和购买合同；安全频率限制用于保护服务的合法利益；必要交易记录用于履行适用法律义务。我们不出售个人信息，不以乐谱建立广告画像。适用法律要求同意的额外处理会另行征求同意。

Account and entitlement data are used to perform the account/purchase contract. Security rate limits support our legitimate interest in protecting the service. Necessary transaction records support applicable legal duties. We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles from scores. Additional processing requiring consent will obtain it separately.

服务商与跨境 / Providers and international transfers

注册验证及密码重置邮件通过 Resend 发送，发送时向其提供收件邮箱和必要邮件内容，不发送乐谱；未完成服务商配置时不发送邮件。账号服务由 Cloudflare Workers 与 D1 托管，安装包由 Cloudflare R2 分发；Creem 独立处理支付业务。服务商可能在您所在地区以外处理数据。运营主体所在法域、适用传输机制及法定交易留存期限需在正式收款前补全，不能将这些待补项视为已完成合规。

Resend delivers registration and password-reset codes using the recipient email and necessary message content, never scores. No messages are sent until provider setup is complete. Cloudflare Workers and D1 host the account service; Cloudflare R2 distributes downloads. Creem separately processes payment services. Providers may process data outside your region. The operator jurisdiction, applicable transfer arrangements and mandatory transaction-retention periods must be finalized before live sales; these outstanding items are not completed compliance.

保存期限与删除 / Retention and deletion

账号资料保留至您删除账号或为提供服务所必需的期间。会话有效期 30 天，到期后由每日清理任务删除；限流记录到期后由每日任务清理。已兑换授权码的摘要保留以防重复使用。账号删除会移除账号、会话、授权及关联订单；Creem 仍可能按其法律义务保留交易资料。本地曲谱、日志及设置不会因卸载程序或删除云端账号而自动删除。

Account records are retained until deletion or while needed to provide the service. Sessions expire after 30 days and are purged daily; expired rate-limit records are also purged daily. Redeemed activation-code hashes are retained to prevent reuse. Account deletion removes the account, sessions, grants and linked checkouts. Creem may retain transaction records to meet its legal duties. Uninstalling the app or deleting an online account does not automatically delete local scores, logs or settings.

您的选择与权利 / Your choices and rights

账号页提供资料导出和删除；删除会使账号授权不可恢复，请先导出必要记录。忘记密码时可使用注册时提供的恢复码重设密码，恢复码只显示一次，也可通过已验证邮箱的验证码重置密码。验证码10分钟有效，仅可使用一次，并受发送与尝试次数限制。依适用法律，您可请求访问、更正、删除、限制处理、可携带资料及向监管机构投诉；联系方式待补全前请勿通过公开 GitHub Issue 提交个人或订单信息。

The account page offers data export and deletion. Deletion makes account entitlements unrecoverable; export necessary records first. A one-time recovery code resets a forgotten password; you can also reset a password using a code sent to your verified email. Email codes expire in 10 minutes, are single-use and subject to delivery/attempt limits. Depending on applicable law, you may have access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and regulatory complaint rights. Do not post personal or order information in public GitHub issues while private contact details remain incomplete.

账号网页仅在当前浏览器会话中保存必要的登录凭据；不包含广告或分析追踪脚本。Windows 凭据使用当前 Windows 用户的 DPAPI 加密保存。服务面向具备订立合同能力的用户，不专门面向儿童。重大变化将更新版本日期，并在适当情况下另行通知。

The account page stores necessary login credentials for the browser session, with no advertising or analytics scripts. Windows credentials are encrypted using the current Windows user’s DPAPI. The service is intended for users able to enter a contract and is not directed at children. Material changes update this notice’s date and will receive additional notice when appropriate.
